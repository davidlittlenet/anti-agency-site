The costs on top of the plan

GoHighLevel bills usage from a wallet. Here is what my own agency billing shows:

My wallet runs about $15 and tops itself up $10 at a time. For a small agency, usage is a small line next to the $297.

Starter or Unlimited?

Starter caps you at 3 sub-accounts. The 4th client pushes you to Unlimited. At 10 clients, Unlimited is about $30 per client a month. At 67, it is under $5. If you plan to grow past 3, start on Unlimited and skip the move later.
