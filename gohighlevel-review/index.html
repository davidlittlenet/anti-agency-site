GoHighLevel Review 2026: From an Agency Running 67 Sub-Accounts

By the person who runs Anti-Agency. I run marketing agencies on GoHighLevel. Prices checked October 6, 2026.

Short answer: GoHighLevel is worth it if you run marketing for several local businesses. It is not worth it for one small business that only needs email. I pay $297 a month for the Unlimited plan and run 67 sub-accounts on it. One sub-account is one client or one brand. For that many accounts, nothing else I know of comes close on price.

What I actually run on it

My sub-accounts are business directories, my own product brands, and client demos. I counted them through the GoHighLevel API on October 6, 2026: 67. Some are tests. Most hold real contacts, real pipelines and real email. I use it for the contact list, the inbox for every site, email sends, missed-call text-back, review requests, and AI voice and chat agents.

What works

  • One bill for many clients. $297 covers every sub-account. My cost per sub-account is under $5 a month.
  • Phone and email at cost. On the Unlimited plan, GoHighLevel rebills phone and email to the client with no markup.
  • AI is cheap when metered. I dropped a $97-a-month unlimited AI add-on. Pay-per-use was cheaper at my volume by a wide margin.

What breaks

  • Email sending domains. Give every sub-account its own sending subdomain. When I shared one across brands, mail from one site stopped reaching inboxes for weeks.
  • Texting setup. US carriers need A2P 10DLC registration first. Plan for days of waiting before the first text goes out.
  • Automations are click-work. You cannot create a workflow through the API. Templates help. Snapshots (a saved copy of a whole sub-account) help more.
  • The learning curve. It does a lot. The first week feels like a cockpit.

Which plan to pick

One business: Starter at $97. An agency with 4 or more clients: Unlimited at $297. Only pick Agency Pro at $497 if you want to sell GoHighLevel to clients as your own software. Full breakdown on the pricing page.
