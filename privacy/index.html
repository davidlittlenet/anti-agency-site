Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026.

This page explains what Anti-Agency collects about you and what we do with it. In short: we collect very little, and we never sell it.

What we collect

  • Server logs. Like every website, our host records your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. We use these only to keep the site running and safe.
  • What you type into a form. For example your name, email, phone number or business name. We use it only to answer you or to do what the form says.

Other companies involved

These services help run the site. Each one gets only what it needs:

  • HighLevel (stores messages and contact details you send us)
  • Google Fonts (serves the fonts; your browser asks Google for them)

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The company you land on may set its own cookies to record that the sale came from us. Its own privacy policy applies there.

Cookies

Our own pages do not need cookies to work. The services listed above may set their own cookies when you use them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

What we never do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share it except with the services listed above, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Write to hello@anti-agency.co and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page first and change the date at the top.
